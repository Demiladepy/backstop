# Backstop design direction

## Design Context

### Users

Backstop is for ordinary people facing a confusing medical insurance denial or
wrongful bill. They may be anxious, tired, short on time, and unfamiliar with
insurance language. Their job is to understand what happened, see trustworthy
evidence, approve only what they are comfortable sending, and follow the case
without learning a new system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, protective, exact — warm and approachable on the landing, precise and
quiet inside the product.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Product (authenticated):** Quiet paper canvas, Obsidian-like three-pane
workspace (left cases, center document, right properties). Literata / Onest /
Source Serif. Document material carries identity.

**Landing / Enter private demo:** August Health warm-clinical language kept
consistent and refined:

- Cream `#f8f3eb` canvas with a soft lavender wash at the top
- Deep Ink `#080331`, warmer stone `#4a4338` for secondary copy
- Primary Indigo `#4865ff` for the only primary CTA role
- Display: **Young Serif** (Reckless Neue substitute) weight 400 only
- UI/body: **Manrope** 400/500 (Saans substitute)
- Floating frosted pill nav, pill buttons, circular portrait-frame orbs
- Tinted feature cards (Ember / Blossom / Forest / Petal) as concentrated color
- White cards with light warm shadow; close band as elevated white panel
- Footer on Midnight Violet `#1b1463`

Positioning line stays verbatim (including its em dash):

> Drafts and sends the appeals you approve — not legal or medical advice.

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Evidence and citations carry identity.
2. **Indigo owns the primary action.** Decorative accents never replace the CTA.
3. **Consequences stay visible.** Draft / approved / sent must not be confusable.
4. **Warmth over sterility.** Cream canvas and warm shadows soften healthcare UI.
5. **Hierarchy by size, not weight.** Serif stays 400; sans uses 400/500 only.
6. **Accessibility is craft.** Visible indigo focus rings, 44px targets, reduced motion.
7. **The demo is a designed journey.** State changes compose the three-minute story.
