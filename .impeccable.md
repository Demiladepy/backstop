# Backstop design direction

## Design Context

### Users

Backstop is for ordinary people facing a confusing medical insurance denial or
wrongful bill. They may be anxious, tired, short on time, and unfamiliar with
insurance language. Their job is to understand what happened, see trustworthy
evidence, approve only what they are comfortable sending, and follow the case
without learning a new system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, protective, exact.

The interface should lower the user's pulse while making every consequential
step legible. It should sound plainspoken and capable, never clinical,
patronizing, combative, or falsely reassuring.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Product (authenticated):** Quiet paper canvas, Obsidian-like three-pane
workspace (left cases, center document, right properties). Literata / Onest /
Source Serif. One scarce accent. No ThoughtLab void-black product chrome.

**Landing / Enter private demo:** Integrated Biosciences light-lab language —
Bone White `#f7f7f5` canvas, Abyssal Ink `#222f30` type, single-weight Inter
Tight display (Aspekta substitute), Roboto Mono labels, Bioluminescent Lime
`#cef79e` only on micro accents (6px dots, 40×40 arrow buttons). Filled CTAs
use Abyssal Ink on light, never lime fills for primary buttons. Flat surfaces,
hairline borders, no shadows. Footer on true `#000000`.

Positioning line stays verbatim (including its em dash):

> Drafts and sends the appeals you approve — not legal or medical advice.

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Evidence and citations carry identity.
2. **Accent rationing.** Lime is a signal lamp, not a brand wash.
3. **Consequences stay visible.** Draft / approved / sent must not be confusable.
4. **Subtraction first.** If it can be removed, remove it.
5. **Accessibility is craft.** WCAG AA, visible focus, 44px targets, reduced motion.
6. **The demo is a designed journey.** State changes compose the three-minute story.
