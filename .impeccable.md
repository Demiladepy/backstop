# Backstop design direction

## Design Context

### Users

Backstop is for ordinary people facing a confusing medical insurance denial or
wrongful bill. They may be anxious, tired, short on time, and unfamiliar with
insurance language. Their job is to understand what happened, see trustworthy
evidence, approve only what they are comfortable sending, and follow the case
without learning a new system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, protective, exact — infrastructure-grade confidence without spectacle.

### Aesthetic Direction

**One light skin for landing and product**, matching Awesomic’s editorial zinc
grid with confetti-orange punctuation.

- Canvas: Paper `#f4f4f5`; elevated cards Snow `#ffffff`
- Type: **DM Sans** (Cosmica substitute) — weight 600 display (56–64px),
  weight 400 body at 14–15px
- Ink: Obsidian `#09090b` headlines and primary CTAs; Graphite `#18181b` body
- Borders: Cloud `#ececee` 1px hairlines — no drop shadows on cards
- Radii: 36px cards, 14px buttons/inputs, 12px badges, 10000px pill only for nav CTAs
- Accent: Ember `#ff5a00` only for scarce badges/chips — never links or fills
- Primary action: Obsidian filled button, white text, inset hairline depth
- Dark feature blocks: Slate `#27272a` for the human-gate band only
- Depth: photographic breakthrough strips (moss/forest), paper grain, SVG noise,
  soft vignettes — never purple glow or heavy drop shadows on cards

Landing structure: sticky white nav · editorial left hero · horizontal peek cards ·
nature breakthrough · dark gate · safety cards · stack strip · forest breakthrough ·
close CTA.

Positioning line stays verbatim (including its em dash):

> Drafts and sends the appeals you approve — not legal or medical advice.

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Evidence and citations carry identity.
2. **99% achromatic.** Ember is punctuation, not a second brand.
3. **Hairlines over shadows.** Elevation is a 1px Cloud border.
4. **One font.** DM Sans for every role from caption to display.
5. **Consequences stay visible.** Draft / approved / sent must not be confusable.
6. **Accessibility is craft.** Visible focus rings, 44px targets, reduced motion.
7. **The demo is a designed journey.** State changes compose the three-minute story.
