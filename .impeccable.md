# Backstop design direction

## Design Context

### Users

Backstop is for ordinary people facing a confusing medical insurance denial or
wrongful bill. They may be anxious, tired, short on time, and unfamiliar with
insurance language. Their job is to understand what happened, see trustworthy
evidence, approve only what they are comfortable sending, and follow the case
without learning a new system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, protective, exact — cinematic on the landing, precise and quiet inside
the product.

### Aesthetic Direction

Midnight cinema projection room, adapted from the Frame.io visual system for
Backstop’s medical-denial workflow.

- Canvas: Obsidian `#0a0a13` with layered cosmic gradients — never flat black
- Type: **Geist** 400/500/600 for all UI and display; **Sono** 400 for all-caps
  section eyebrows only
- Accent: Iris Glow `#6199f6` for icons, eyebrows, links, and active states
- Borders: Twilight `#4f4f80` halos on product frames; Charcoal `#2a2a32` hairlines
- Primary action: Carbon Vellum pill, Obsidian text — no colored CTA
- Product frames: 10px radius, 1px twilight border, no button drop shadows
- Display headlines stay weight 400; size and tracking do the work

Positioning line stays verbatim (including its em dash):

> Drafts and sends the appeals you approve — not legal or medical advice.

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Evidence lives in twilight-framed surfaces.
2. **One blue, used surgically.** Iris Glow never becomes a fill or a second brand.
3. **Consequences stay visible.** Draft / approved / sent must not be confusable.
4. **Pills frame action; type frames the story.** 100px radius on buttons only.
5. **Hierarchy by size, not weight.** Display stays 400; UI uses 400/500.
6. **Accessibility is craft.** Visible iris focus rings, 44px targets, reduced motion.
7. **The demo is a designed journey.** State changes compose the three-minute story.
