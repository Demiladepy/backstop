# Backstop design direction

## Design Context

### Users

Backstop is for ordinary people facing a confusing medical insurance denial or
wrongful bill. They may be anxious, tired, short on time, and unfamiliar with
insurance language. Their job is to understand what happened, see trustworthy
evidence, approve only what they are comfortable sending, and follow the case
without learning a new system.

### Brand Personality

Calm, protective, exact — expressed through restraint: void canvas, monumental
type, and one scarce crimson action.

### Aesthetic Direction

**ThoughtLab language for Backstop:** void-black cathedral, oversized frost
display type, ash body text, and a single Crimson Signal pill for the primary
action. Hierarchy comes from scale and whitespace, not cards, shadows, or
gradients.

Keep Obsidian-like workspace grammar inside the product (left cases, center
document, right properties) on the void canvas. Do not add a second accent,
glow, glass, or filled cards.

References (adapt, do not copy): ThoughtLab, Instrument, Locomotive zero-elevation.

### Design Principles

1. **The document is the interface.** Evidence and citations carry identity.
2. **One crimson pulse.** Primary CTA only; nowhere else.
3. **Consequences stay visible.** Draft / approved / sent must not be confusable.
4. **Subtraction first.** If it can be removed, remove it.
5. **Accessibility is craft.** WCAG AA, visible focus, 44px targets, reduced motion.
6. **The demo is a designed journey.** State changes compose the three-minute story.

### System Rules

- ThoughtLab tokens: Void `#000000`, Ash `#cccccc`, Frost `#ffffff`,
  Graphite `#4c4c4c`, Crimson Signal `#fc1c46`.
- Sole family: Space Grotesk (Sui substitute).
- Pill radius only on buttons/tags; 0 on cards and inputs.
- No box-shadow elevation stack.
- Positioning line stays verbatim (including its em dash).
