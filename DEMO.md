# Demo script (under 3 minutes)

Positioning line, said once:

> Drafts and sends the appeals you approve — not legal or medical advice.

0:00–0:20 — Open the live site. Enter the private demo. Start a sample case
with the fictional denial letter.

0:20–1:00 — Watch parse, policy research, and the cited draft appear live.

1:00–1:40 — Show citations. Approve. The email sends. Show the thread and
audit timeline.

1:40–2:20 — Watch tab: deadline catch or public form filled and stopped
before submit. Say: it never touches a password or card.

2:20–2:50 — Close on the live board. Same engine, one shipped case type.
